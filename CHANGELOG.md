# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (8 November 2026)

First public release.

- Time-based greeting, live clock and day progress bar
- 3D protein of the day with cartoon, surface and stick views
- Animated DNA helix
- Search across Google, Scholar, PubMed, UniProt, PDB, AlphaFold DB, NCBI, PubChem, arXiv, Hugging Face and GitHub
- Quick links for genomics, proteins, docking and machine learning
- AI assistants and Google apps sections
- Lab notes saved in the browser and a daily research tip
- Light and dark themes, mobile-friendly layout
